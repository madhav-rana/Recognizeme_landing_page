About RecognizeMe

We are a team of passionate developers building the future of classroom attendance using cutting-edge AI, facial recognition, and voice biometrics.

— Our Mission —

Making attendance effortless for every educator

Traditional attendance is slow, error-prone, and a waste of precious class time. RecognizeMe was built to eliminate that friction — giving teachers back their time and students a seamless, modern experience.

We believe technology should work for educators, not against them. That's why our system is fast, accurate, and requires zero technical expertise to use.
